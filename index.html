OUR INSTITUTION

A strong foundation for every learner.

Discover our curriculum, school leadership and commitment to modern, student-centred education.

⌖
Located in Mazar-i-Sharif

Kocha Siyagerd, Mazar-i-Sharif, Balkh Province, Afghanistan

▤

National & Supplementary Curriculum

We follow the official national curriculum of Afghanistan’s Ministry of Education, enriched with supplementary Oxford and Maref programs to support language learning, core sciences and digital skills.

⌂

Primary to High School

Our educational pathway spans primary, middle and high school levels, with teaching and assessment guided by applicable education standards.

✦

Leadership & Teaching

Dedicated school administration coordinates daily operations, curriculum delivery, staff schedules, examinations and official reporting to the Balkh Directorate of Education. Experienced educators support progress in languages, mathematics and natural sciences.

◎

Student-Centred Learning

We value a supportive learning environment, modern teaching aids, active participation and regular communication with parents and guardians.

▣

Organised School Services

Our administrative processes support enrollment records, Detailed Marks Certificates (DMC), student assessment tracking and official communication.

↗

Progress & Development

We aim to strengthen academic knowledge, communication, scientific thinking and responsible digital skills while encouraging every learner to keep improving.

✧

Our Growth & Identity

The school grew from New Al-Falah High School No. 2 into Al-Falah Furqan Private High School, reflecting a long-term commitment to trusted education, continued development and a stronger institutional identity.

✓

Academic Supervision

Principal-led coordination and staff development support curriculum completion, punctuality, classroom management and student progress. Structured examinations and continuous assessment help prepare learners for the next stage of education.

▣

Digital School Services

The school is working to organize student enrollment, attendance and performance records through digital systems, alongside online announcements and communication tools for families.

✦

Professional School Branding

The school’s visual identity is used across official letterheads, emblems, examination cards, DMCs, merit certificates and parent notices, with dark red, navy blue and metallic gold featured in institutional materials.
